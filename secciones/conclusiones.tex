\section{Conclusions and Future Work}

This work presents a fuzzy logic-based continuous assessment model tailored for remote and hybrid learning environments. The model integrates multiple variables to provide fair, transparent, and learning-oriented grading. Results show improved alignment with student engagement and satisfaction compared to traditional methods \cite{Lopez2021, Santos2022}.

Future research will focus on refining the fuzzy rules, expanding the model to other educational contexts, and exploring integration with adaptive learning technologies \cite{Smith2023}.
