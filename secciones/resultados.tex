\section{Results}

The fuzzy grading model was tested using both simulated and real student data from remote courses. The results indicate that the model provides more nuanced and equitable grades compared to traditional methods. Key findings include:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Increased alignment between grades and student engagement \cite{Lopez2021}
	\item Greater transparency and acceptance among students \cite{Nguyen2020}
	\item Reduction in grade disputes and perceived unfairness \cite{Santos2022}
\end{itemize}
